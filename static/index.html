How to use Virtual Judge

One person drives this from one laptop. A team pitches, you record it, and about thirty seconds later the room hears the review read back.

Before the first team

  1. Check the keys. Open /api/health?verify=1. It makes a live call to each provider. A key being present is not the same as a key that works, and you want to find that out now rather than with a team standing in front of you.
  2. Create the event. Pick it from the Event dropdown, or hit New Event. Everything you record is filed under it.
  3. Do one dry run. Record thirty seconds of yourself and judge it. This is the only way to catch a mic problem before it costs you a team.

For each team

  1. Type the team name in the Judge tab.
  2. Hit Start Recording as they begin. The waveform confirms audio is actually reaching the machine.
  3. Hit Stop & Judge when they finish. Transcription, scoring and the spoken review run in one pass and take roughly thirty seconds.
  4. Let the review play. Scores and reasoning appear on screen while the voice reads them out. That pause is a good moment for applause and swapping teams over.

Once everyone has pitched

  1. Open the Finalist tab and hit Run Finalist Round. Every team is compared against every other, and the top three are announced out loud.

After the room clears

  1. Generate PRFAQs from the Submissions tab. Each team gets a Working Backwards document written from their own pitch: the product as though it had already launched, then the questions they cannot answer yet and a ledger grading every claim they made against what they actually showed.
  2. Export to Folder, then send each team their own file. Most teams get more out of the ledger than out of where they placed.

Two things that bite. Do not run PRFAQs between teams, because they take far longer than a room will sit through. And a laptop mic picks up the whole room, so a USB mic pointed at the presenter is the single biggest quality win available to you.
